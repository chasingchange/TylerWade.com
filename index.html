Tyler Wade is a life coach, director, founder, author, and model based in Austin, Texas. His work is driven by a belief that the strongest stories, both on the page and in real life, are built through growth, discipline, and the courage to embrace change.

As the founder of Chasing Change, Tyler coaches ambitious professionals in developing their health, confidence, and personal direction through sustainable fitness and lifestyle transformation. His coaching philosophy extends beyond physical results, emphasizing the holistic habits and mindset that create lasting change.

Alongside his work as a coach, Tyler is an author developing original speculative fiction, screenplays, and plays that explore identity, ambition, and the complexities of the human experience. His creative background shapes the way he approaches every collaboration, bringing curiosity, intention, and authenticity to his work in front of the camera.

Whether partnering with brands, coaching clients, or creating original stories, Tyler is passionate about helping people see what they're capable of and creating work that leaves a lasting impact for decades to come.
